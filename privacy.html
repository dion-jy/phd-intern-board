Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-20

What this site keeps about you

Nothing. There are no accounts, no forms and no first-party cookies. The listings are a static page; filtering and searching happen in your browser and are not sent anywhere.

The view counter

Each page load increments a single anonymous number held by Abacus. The request carries no identifier and sets no cookie; like any web request it reaches Abacus's server with your IP address, which this site never sees.

Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. When it does:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • How Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before any advertising cookie is set.

Links out

Every listing links to the employer's own careers page, and the announcements strip links to X. Those sites have their own privacy policies; this one does not cover them.
